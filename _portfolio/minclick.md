An iPhone app for logging live poker. Save your two cards at the table, finish the hand when play slows down, and review the whole session afterwards.

- On-device hole-card recognition and session recordings with hand-start markers (Pro)
- Editable hand histories, PnL, hourly rate and win-rate charts, filters, and comparison reports, all free
- Bankrolls, home-game ledgers, CSV import and export, and local JSON backups
- Local first: core records stay on the phone, and an account is only needed for backup, sync, or a private friend feed
